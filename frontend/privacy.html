Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Welcome to Plexudo ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to transparency, user privacy, and open access. Plexudo operates as a 100% login-free, public creator platform. You can use our YouTube SEO and channel analytics tools immediately without account registration, sign-up, personal profile setup, or passwords. This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled across our website at https://plexudo.vercel.app.

1. Information We Process

Because Plexudo is completely anonymous and login-free, we do not collect names, passwords, email addresses, or personal user profiles. We process only:

  • Public Search & SEO Queries: Public keywords, hashtags, video URLs, and channel identifiers (@handles or channel IDs) that you submit to calculate SEO scores, tags, and audience sentiment.
  • Operational & Security Data: Standard network metadata (including client IP addresses and user-agent strings) processed strictly to enforce rate limits, detect bot abuse, and ensure high availability.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Plexudo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. YouTube API Services

Plexudo utilizes the official YouTube Data API v3 to retrieve public metadata, view counts, engagement ratios, and keyword tags for video and channel optimization. By using Plexudo's YouTube features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Plexudo only accesses publicly available YouTube data and does not require or store personal Google OAuth credentials.

3. How We Use Information

  • To compute real-time 50/50 SEO scores, competitor benchmarks, and engagement analytics.
  • To generate context-aware YouTube titles, descriptions, and hashtags via AI.
  • To enforce IP-based rate limiting and protect public creator tools against automated scraping, denial-of-service, and abuse.
  • To monitor platform health, system latency, and cache efficiency.

4. Advertising

Plexudo uses Google AdSense to display advertisements on public pages of this website. Google AdSense is operated by Google LLC. As part of this service:

  • Google may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • We do not control the content of Google ads, and we do not receive individually identifiable information about you from Google as part of this service.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use Google's advertising services, please visit: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

5. AI Processing

Plexudo uses third-party AI services to generate YouTube title suggestions, content recommendations, and SEO scoring insights. Specifically:

  • Groq AI (Groq Inc.): When you use AI-powered title generation or keyword suggestions, the keyword or topic you submit (along with anonymised context) is sent to Groq's API for processing. We do not send personal account information, email addresses, or credentials to Groq.
  • Groq processes your submitted content in accordance with Groq's Privacy Policy.
  • You should not submit sensitive personal information (such as passwords, financial details, or private personal data) in keyword or content fields.

6. Security, Rate Limiting & Infrastructure

We take infrastructure security and abuse mitigation seriously to guarantee free, continuous access for all creators:

  • No-Login Architecture: No passwords, session tokens, or personal identifiers are stored or required.
  • Server-Side Rate Limiting: Sliding-window rate limiters monitor endpoint consumption per IP address (e.g., 100 requests/minute for standard tools, 30 requests/minute for AI consultations) to prevent denial-of-service and quota exhaustion.
  • Backend-Only Secrets: All API keys (YouTube Data API v3, Groq API) are strictly confined to backend server environments and are never transmitted to or accessible from browser clients.
  • Encrypted Communications: All data in transit is encrypted using modern HTTPS / TLS 1.3 standards.

7. Data Retention & User Rights

Because Plexudo does not maintain user accounts or personal profiles, there is no persistent account profile data to manage or delete. Analysis metrics are computed on-the-fly. Operational logs contain only public search terms and anonymized operational timestamps, purged on a rolling schedule.
