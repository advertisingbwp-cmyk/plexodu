Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

Welcome to Plexudo ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our website at https://plexudo.vercel.app and our YouTube SEO tools.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when creating an account, authenticating via Google OAuth, or using our analytics tools:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hash, account preferences.
  • Google & YouTube Profile Data: When you connect your Google/YouTube account, we receive your email address, channel name, profile picture, and public channel metrics via official Google OAuth API scopes.
  • Search & SEO Queries: Keywords, topics, and video metadata you analyze to provide real-time SEO scoring and title recommendations.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Plexudo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. YouTube API Services

Plexudo utilizes the official YouTube Data API v3. By using Plexudo's YouTube features, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke Plexudo's access to your data at any time via the Google Security Settings page.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Plexudo YouTube SEO Studio and creator tools.
  • To authenticate your session securely and synchronize channel analytics.
  • To generate truthful, context-aware YouTube titles and descriptions.
  • To prevent abuse, bot activity, and ensure service reliability.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, secure HTTP-only cookies, and hashed password storage (PBKDF2/SHA-256). We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers.

5. Account Deletion & Data Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete your account and all associated trend histories permanently at any time directly through the Account Settings → Danger Zone in your Plexudo dashboard.
